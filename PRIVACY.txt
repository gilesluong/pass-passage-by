PRIVACY — LOCAL PREVIEW
16 September 2026

Pass Passage By! stores your draft locally in Application Support/Passage. The legacy folder name is retained to preserve existing work. Appearance and zoom preferences are stored in macOS UserDefaults, and macOS records recent documents.

This build does not add analytics, advertising, account registration, cloud sync or a remote AI service. Import and export use files you select. Dictionary lookups are passed to macOS Dictionary Services; dictionary availability and behavior depend on your Mac and installed dictionaries.

JSON documents may contain your writing, annotations and embedded task images. MD/TXT exports contain text only. You choose where to save and share them. Other software, system backups or sync services may process files in those locations under their own policies.

Sparkle checks the configured GitHub Releases HTTPS feed for signed updates. GitHub receives normal download/request metadata; essays and images are not included. You can disable automatic checks in Settings → Updates.

Scans are processed locally using Apple Vision. Original images are retained in the document only when Keep source images is selected. Optional annotation suggestions use the on-device Apple Intelligence model when available. The app does not download AI models automatically or send scans to a remote AI service. My writing stores documents in Application Support/Passage/Library. Copy agent brief puts selected writing and skill instructions on the clipboard; your chosen external agent has its own privacy policy.

Closing the app does not delete its local draft. Keep exports of work you need to retain. This notice describes this local preview, not future hosted services.
