## Rehaul of Editor, Annotations, Dictionary & Gestures

- **Margin Annotations**: Resolved vertical overlapping. Annotations are truncated to 3 lines with a Liquid Glass popover preview ("View full note ↗") to prevent long citations from monopolizing the margin.
- **Apple Intelligence & Writing Insight**: Real-time writing and coherence suggestions on hover/click when holding `fn`.
- **Collapsible Task Prompt**: Replaced text toggle with animated Liquid Glass chevron button (0.28s slide animation) and increased task prompt font size to >= 20pt.
- **Apple Dictionary Layout**: Redesigned dictionary view to match Apple's native Dictionary.app typography with dividers, bold 22pt headword, phonetic transcript, italic part of speech, numbered definitions, and synonyms block.
- **Direct Semantic Zoom**: Direct navigation between Essay and Word definition (`[0, 3]`), skipping intermediate steps. Renamed settings section to Gestures.

Apple Silicon, macOS 13+. Sparkle-signed update; development ad-hoc app signing, not Apple-notarized.
