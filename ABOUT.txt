Let the words take you somewhere.

A quiet native writing space for students, teachers and curious readers. Move from the whole argument to a single word, look it up in your Mac’s dictionaries, and keep your thoughts connected in the margins.

Write in Markdown. Put a note inside {braces} to connect it to a margin. Choose Student, Teacher or Custom in Settings → Zoom. Your source remains editable text.

Built for macOS • Local-first • No account required

This is an independent preview, not affiliated with Ulysses, Apple or IELTS. Markdown compatibility is partial. Dictionary text comes from dictionaries installed on your Mac.
